Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is the standard for building LLM applications that reason over private, up-to-date data. Instead of relying solely on the model's static training weights, RAG retrieves relevant facts from your own documents and injects them directly into the prompt as context.

This guide moves beyond simple vector search to explore the advanced patterns used in production. The "right" RAG implementation is never one-size-fits-all; it depends entirely on your document density, query complexity, and latency requirements.

### Foundational
- [Basic RAG](/rag/foundational/basic-rag) — The baseline pattern of chunking, embedding, and retrieving.
- [Chunk Sizes](/rag/foundational/chunk-sizes.mdx) — Finding the optimal balance between precision and context.
- [Proposition Chunking](/rag/foundational/proposition-chunking.mdx) — Deconstructing text into atomic factual statements for higher accuracy.
- [Reliable RAG](/rag/foundational/reliable-rag.mdx) — Adding a verification loop to catch and eliminate hallucinations.

### Query Enhancement
- [HyDE](/rag/query-enhancement/hyde.mdx) — Using hypothetical answers to bridge the gap between questions and documents.
- [HyPE](/rag/query-enhancement/hype.mdx) — Generating hypothetical questions at ingest time for faster, more relevant search.
- [Query Transformations](/rag/query-enhancement/query-transformations.mdx) — Rewriting user queries into multiple perspectives to handle ambiguity.

### Context Enrichment
- [Semantic Chunking](/rag/context-enrichment/semantic-chunking.mdx) — Splitting documents at semantic boundaries instead of fixed counts.
- [Contextual Compression](/rag/context-enrichment/contextual-compression.mdx) — Summarizing retrieved chunks to fit more relevant signal into the window.
- [Contextual Headers](/rag/context-enrichment/contextual-headers.mdx) — Preserving structure by prepending metadata to every chunk.
- [Relevant Segment Extraction](/rag/context-enrichment/relevant-segment-extraction.mdx) — Isolating exact sentences within a chunk that answer the query.
- [Context Window Enhancement](/rag/context-enrichment/context-window-enhancement.mdx) — Including neighboring chunks to provide wider document context.
- [Document Augmentation](/rag/context-enrichment/document-augmentation.mdx) — Enriching chunks with generated metadata during ingestion.
