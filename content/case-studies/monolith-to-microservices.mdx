## The Problem
The existing platform was built tightly coupled. As traffic grew during holiday seasons, the entire application would buckle under database locking issues, causing checkout failures.

## The Solution
We decided to strangle the monolith. 
1. **Frontend Modernization**: Moved to Next.js App Router for edge caching.
2. **Backend Splitting**: Separated the Inventory, Cart, and Checkout into independent microservices.

## The Results
- **99.99% Uptime** during Black Friday.
- **40% Reduction** in cloud compute costs.
- **LCP** improved from 4.2s to 1.1s.
